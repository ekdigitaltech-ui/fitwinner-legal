Part A — Privacy

1. Person in charge of the protection of personal information

Under section 3.1 of the Québec Act and Principle 1 of PIPEDA, the person in charge of the protection of personal information (privacy officer) is:

Enis Karpuz, Director, EK Digital Technologies OÜ Email: enis@ekdigital.tech (subject line: "Privacy officer") Address: Sepapaja tn 6, Lasnamäe linnaosa, 15551 Tallinn, Harju maakond, Estonia

This person answers access and rectification requests, complaints and questions about this supplement, and decides on confidentiality incidents.

2. What we collect, why, and how

  • Information you enter in the app: birth year, biological sex, height, weight, goal, activity and training details, meal, workout and weigh-in logs, the daily wellness check-in. Purpose: to calculate your calorie, macronutrient and form-score figures and keep your history. This information stays on your device, except for the AI payload described below, which needs your consent, and any backup file you choose to save yourself (section 4).
  • Sensitive information, only with your express consent: health-screening answers and Apple Health readings. Purpose: to switch unsafe features off for you and to adjust your daily figures. Consent is requested separately, for this purpose only, and the box is never pre-ticked (Québec Act s. 12 and 14).
  • Only with your express consent: the de-identified AI payload described in Privacy Notice section 6.2. We do not collect your email address and send no marketing emails.
  • Not collected: location, contacts, photos, camera roll, advertising identifiers, government identifiers, biometric data.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising.

3. Functions that profile you (Québec Act s. 8.1)

The app evaluates characteristics related to your health and behaviour: it calculates a calorie target, macronutrient targets and a daily form (recovery) score from what you enter and, if you allow it, from Apple Health. This is the service you ask the app for. The calculation runs on your device with fixed formulas. Unless you give the "AI service abroad" consent, the result never leaves your device; with that consent, your form-score series is sent in de-identified form for the AI interpretation (section 6). We do not use the result for advertising or marketing.

Functions that are off until you switch them on: AI interpretations (the "AI service abroad" consent), use of health-screening answers (the health-data consent), and reading Apple Health (the iOS permission). You activate or deactivate each of them at any time under Profile › Your consents or in iOS Settings. The app has no function that identifies or locates you.

4. Privacy by default (Québec Act s. 9.1)

Every optional setting that sends or reads personal information is off by default: AI, health data and Apple Health all require your action. There is no iCloud or other cloud sync.

Backup file: nothing is backed up automatically. A copy of your records exists outside the device only if you save a backup file under Profile › My data, and you choose where it goes. The file is password-protected by default; we cannot read it and cannot recover the password. We do not receive the file.

5. Automated processing

Your calorie, macronutrient and form-score figures are produced automatically on your device. They are suggestions for you, not decisions made about you by us. On request we tell you what information was used and the main factors behind a figure, and you can correct your inputs at any time in the app. You can also send us your observations at the address in section 1 (Québec Act s. 12.1).

6. Communication outside Québec and outside Canada

Our enterprise is established in Estonia. When you allow AI, information is communicated outside Québec and outside Canada:

  • Supabase, Inc. (United States; database in Japan; functions at global edge locations): cloud infrastructure for the AI functions; three technical records described in Privacy Notice section 5.3.
  • Anthropic, PBC (United States): AI interpretation of the de-identified payload.

Before any communication outside Québec we carried out a privacy impact assessment under section 17 of the Québec Act. It considered the sensitivity of the information, the purpose, the safeguards and the legal framework of the United States and Japan. The communication takes place under written data-processing agreements with each provider. While your information is abroad, it is subject to the laws of that country, and courts, law-enforcement or national-security authorities there may be able to access it.

7. Retention and destruction

Retention periods are those in Privacy Notice section 8: records on your device until you delete them; analysis requests are not stored; the abuse counter at most 2 days; the safety audit trail 90 days. When the purpose is achieved, information is destroyed or anonymized.

8. Your rights

You may, free of charge:

  • access the personal information we hold about you and learn how it is used and to whom it was communicated;
  • have it corrected if it is inaccurate, incomplete or ambiguous;
  • withdraw any consent at any time (Profile › Your consents), subject to the consequences explained in Privacy Notice section 7;
  • receive computerized personal information you provided in a structured, commonly used technological format (the JSON export under Profile › My data, Québec Act s. 27);
  • ask that dissemination of your personal information cease, or that a hyperlink be de-indexed (Québec Act s. 28.1); we do not publish any personal information about you.

Most of your information lives only on your device, so access, export and deletion work instantly in the app. Written requests go to the person in charge (section 1) and are answered within 30 days. If we refuse a request, we give you the reasons in writing and tell you how to contest the refusal; Québec residents may ask the Commission d'accès à l'information to examine the disagreement within 30 days of the refusal.

9. Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to:

  • Québec residents: Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (cai.gouv.qc.ca);
  • all Canadian residents: Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca);
  • Alberta and British Columbia residents: the Information and Privacy Commissioner of your province (oipc.ab.ca, oipc.bc.ca).

10. Confidentiality incidents

We keep a register of every confidentiality incident. If an incident presents a risk of serious injury (Québec) or a real risk of significant harm (PIPEDA, Alberta), we promptly notify the Commission d'accès à l'information, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the Alberta Commissioner, as applicable, and the persons concerned.

11. Our governance of personal information (Québec Act s. 3.2)

  • Roles: the person in charge (section 1) approves this supplement and decides on requests and incidents; the developer applies the technical measures.
  • Retention and destruction: fixed periods (section 7), enforced in code and reviewed every year.
  • Requests and complaints: received by email, logged, answered within 30 days, with the complaint routes in section 9.
  • Incidents: a written response procedure with a register kept for at least five years.
  • Impact assessments: carried out before any new information system, new recipient or new communication outside Québec; they are updated at every material change.
  • Awareness: everyone with access to the service's infrastructure follows these rules and uses two-factor authentication.

Ces renseignements restent sur votre appareil, sauf les données envoyées à l'IA décrites ci-dessous, qui exigent votre consentement, et tout fichier de sauvegarde que vous choisissez d'enregistrer vous-même (section 4).

3. Fonctions de profilage (Loi québécoise, art. 8.1)

L'application évalue des caractéristiques liées à votre santé et à votre comportement : elle calcule une cible calorique, des cibles de macronutriments et un indice quotidien de forme (récupération) à partir de ce que vous saisissez et, si vous l'autorisez, d'Apple Santé. C'est le service que vous demandez à l'application. Le calcul s'effectue sur votre appareil selon des formules fixes. Sans le consentement « service d'IA à l'étranger », le résultat ne quitte jamais votre appareil; avec ce consentement, la série de vos indices de forme est envoyée sous forme dépersonnalisée pour l'interprétation par IA (section 6). Nous n'utilisons le résultat ni pour la publicité ni pour le marketing.

Fonctions désactivées tant que vous ne les activez pas : les interprétations par IA (consentement « service d'IA à l'étranger »), l'utilisation des réponses au questionnaire de santé (consentement relatif aux données de santé), et la lecture d'Apple Santé (autorisation iOS). Vous activez ou désactivez chacune d'elles en tout temps sous Profile › Your consents (interface en anglais) ou dans les Réglages iOS. L'application ne comporte aucune fonction permettant de vous identifier ou de vous localiser.

4. Confidentialité par défaut (Loi québécoise, art. 9.1)

Tout paramètre facultatif qui transmet ou lit des renseignements personnels est désactivé par défaut : l'IA, les données de santé et Apple Santé exigent tous une action de votre part. Il n'y a ni iCloud ni autre synchronisation infonuagique.

Fichier de sauvegarde : rien n'est sauvegardé automatiquement. Une copie de vos données n'existe hors de l'appareil que si vous enregistrez un fichier de sauvegarde sous Profile › My data, et vous choisissez où il va. Le fichier est protégé par un mot de passe par défaut; nous ne pouvons ni le lire ni récupérer le mot de passe. Nous ne recevons pas le fichier.
