Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Version: 1.1 · Effective: 27 September 2026 · Last updated: 27 September 2026 · Regulated entity: EK Digital Technologies OÜ, Sepapaja tn 6, 15551 Tallinn, Estonia (Äriregister 17280400)

This policy is published for residents of Washington (My Health My Data Act, RCW 19.373), Nevada (Senate Bill 370, NRS 603A) and Connecticut (Connecticut Data Privacy Act, consumer health data provisions), and for any other person who wants to know exactly how the FitWinner app handles consumer health data. It supplements our Privacy Policy and Privacy Notice. For consumer health data, this policy prevails over any inconsistent statement in those documents.

"Consumer health data" means personal information that is linked or reasonably linkable to you and that identifies your past, present or future physical or mental health status, including the categories listed in section 1.

1. Categories of consumer health data we collect, and why

FitWinner is a device-first app. Every category below is collected only with your separate, affirmative, opt-in consent given in the app, and is stored on your device (and, if you turn on iCloud backup, in your own private iCloud database that we cannot read).

Category Examples Purpose
Health-screening answers pregnancy or breastfeeding; kidney disease; high blood pressure; blood-thinner (anticoagulant) use; regular medication (yes/no); two eating-behaviour screening questions; if you declared kidney disease, the daily protein target you entered from your clinician To switch unsafe features off for you (for example no calorie deficit during pregnancy or after an eating-disorder signal; no macronutrient targets after a kidney-disease declaration; no caffeine suggestions with high blood pressure). Your answers never produce a medical target for you.
Apple Health readings (read-only) active energy, steps, workouts, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, sleep To adjust your daily calorie target and to compute your daily form (recovery) score on your device.
Body and fitness information you enter birth year, biological sex, height, weight, optional body-fat percentage, goal, training and occupation details, food and workout logs, daily wellness answers (sleep, fatigue, soreness, stress), an optional short check-in note To calculate calorie, macronutrient and hydration targets and the form score on your device; to keep your history.
Foods you avoid and disliked foods (free text) "peanut", "lactose" To remove matching items from meal suggestions. We do not ask why and infer no allergy, religion or belief.
Derived indicators daily form score; an eating-safety signal derived from your own logs To narrow suggestions in the safe direction. The eating-safety signal is recomputed each time and is never stored.

We do not collect precise geolocation, contacts, photos, biometric identifiers, genetic data, advertising identifiers or government identifiers. We do not use geofencing. We do not knowingly collect consumer health data from anyone under 18; the app is rated 18+ and refuses registration below that age.

2. Categories of sources

  • You: what you type or answer in the app.
  • Apple Health on your device: only the data types you authorise in iOS Settings, read-only.

We do not purchase, license or otherwise obtain consumer health data from third parties, affiliates or data brokers.

3. Categories of consumer health data we share, and with whom

We do not sell consumer health data and have never sold it. We do not share it with advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers or affiliates (we have none).

Sharing happens only if, and only while, you give the separate "AI service abroad" consent in the app. That consent is distinct from your consent to collect the data. When it is active, a de-identified request is sent, at the moment you ask for an interpretation, through our cloud provider to our AI provider:

Recipient (specific third party) Role What it receives
Supabase, Inc. (USA; database region Tokyo, Japan) cloud infrastructure, processor the de-identified request in transit; an IP-based abuse counter kept at most 2 days; an identity-free safety audit trail kept 90 days
Anthropic, PBC (USA) AI model provider, processor the de-identified request; may retain inputs for a limited period for abuse monitoring under its own policy

Categories shared in that request: your form-score series and its components, summaries of your last 28 days of training, calendar events, goal, sport, experience level, language, your optional check-in note, contraindication tags derived from the health screening (for example "kidney disease"), and — for food recognition — the food name and portion you typed plus your last 20 food names. The request carries no name, e-mail, user or device identifier, age, sex, height, weight or calorie target. Our server keeps no copy of the request.

Apple Inc. hosts your iCloud backup as your own service provider under your Apple Account; we do not transmit data to Apple and cannot read that backup.

4. Retention

On your device: until you delete it or delete the app. iCloud: under your control. Our servers: no consumer health data is stored; the IP abuse counter is deleted after at most 2 days and the identity-free audit trail after 90 days.

5. How we protect consumer health data

Data stays on your device under iOS app sandboxing; optional backups go to your private iCloud database, which Apple encrypts and we cannot access. Requests to our server carry no identifiers and pass an automated de-identification gate on the device and again on the server. Access to our cloud console is limited to the company's director and protected by two-factor authentication.

6. Your rights and how to exercise them

You can, at any time and free of charge:

  • Confirm and access: see everything the app holds about you and export it as a file — Profile › My data › Export.
  • Delete: delete all your data — Profile › My data › Delete all my data. This removes it from the device and from your iCloud backup. Because our servers hold no copy and the AI provider receives only de-identified requests, there is nothing further for us or for our processors to delete; on request we confirm this in writing within the periods below.
  • Withdraw consent: for health-screening data or for the AI service — Profile › My consents. Withdrawal takes effect immediately and deletes AI-generated text stored on your device.
  • Obtain the list of third parties with whom your consumer health data has been shared: see section 3; we will also provide it on request.
  • Ask questions or exercise any right by e-mail to ekdigitaltech@gmail.com (subject line "Health data request"). We answer within 45 days of receipt; if we need more time we tell you before the 45 days end and may take up to 45 additional days. Where a shorter period applies to you (30 days under KVKK, one month under GDPR) we respect it.
  • Appeal a decision by replying to our answer within 45 days; we re-examine it and respond in writing within 45 days, including how to contact your state attorney general: Washington State Office of the Attorney General, Nevada Office of the Attorney General, Connecticut Office of the Attorney General.

We will not deny goods or services, charge different prices or provide a different level of service because you exercised a right.

7. Changes to this policy

We change this policy when the app's handling of consumer health data changes and update the version, effective date and "last updated" line at the top. Material changes are announced in the app before they take effect and, where required, we ask for your consent again.
