Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Version: 1.0 · Effective: 27 September 2026 · Controller: EK Digital Technologies OÜ, Tallinn, Estonia (Äriregister 17280400)

This policy is provided for residents of Washington (My Health My Data Act, RCW 19.373), Nevada (SB 370) and Connecticut (Consumer Data Privacy Act, consumer health data provisions), and for any other user who wants to know exactly how FitWinner handles health-related data. It supplements our Privacy Policy and Privacy Notice; where they differ, this policy governs consumer health data.

1. What consumer health data we collect

FitWinner is a device-first app. The following categories are collected only with your separate, opt-in consent given in the app, and are stored on your device (and, if you enable it, in your own private iCloud database that we cannot read):

  • Health-screening answers: pregnancy or breastfeeding; kidney disease; high blood pressure; blood-thinner (anticoagulant) use; regular medication (yes/no); two eating-behaviour screening questions. If you declared kidney disease, the daily protein target you entered from your clinician.
  • Apple Health readings (read-only, never written back): active energy, steps, workouts, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, sleep.
  • Body and fitness data you type: birth year, biological sex, height, weight, body-fat percentage (optional), goal, training and occupation details, food and workout logs, daily wellness answers (sleep, fatigue, soreness, stress) and an optional short check-in note.
  • Foods you avoid (free text) and disliked foods. We do not ask why; no allergy, religion or belief category is inferred.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or any government identifier. We do not use geofencing.

2. Sources

All consumer health data comes from you (what you type or answer) or from Apple Health on your device, with your permission in iOS Settings. We do not buy or receive health data from third parties.

3. Why we collect it (purposes)

  • To calculate your calorie, macronutrient and hydration targets and your daily form score on your device.
  • To switch unsafe features off for you (for example: no calorie deficit during pregnancy or after an eating-disorder signal; no macro targets after a kidney-disease declaration; no caffeine suggestions with high blood pressure). Health answers never produce a medical target for you.
  • Only if you separately consent: to obtain interpretive text from an AI service (see section 4).

We do not use consumer health data for advertising, marketing, profiling for third parties, or any purpose not listed here.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell consumer health data. We do not share it with data brokers, advertisers or analytics providers.

The only sharing happens if, and only while, you give the separate "AI service abroad" consent in the app. In that case a de-identified payload is sent through our cloud provider (Supabase, Inc., USA; database in Japan) to the AI provider (Anthropic, PBC, USA) to generate explanatory text. The payload contains: your form-score series and its components, summaries of your last 28 days of training, calendar events, goal, sport, experience level, language, your optional check-in note, contraindication tags derived from the health screening (for example "kidney disease"), and — for food recognition — the food name and portion you typed plus your last 20 food names. It contains no name, e-mail, user or device identifier, age, sex, height, weight or calorie target. Our server keeps no copy of the request; Anthropic may retain inputs for a limited period for abuse monitoring under its own policy.

You can withdraw that consent at any time under Profile › My consents; withdrawal takes effect immediately and deletes AI-generated text stored on your device.

Categories of recipients: cloud infrastructure provider (processor), AI service provider (processor). Affiliates: none.

5. How we protect it

Data stays on your device under iOS app sandboxing; optional backups go to your private iCloud database, which Apple encrypts and we cannot access. Requests to our server carry no identifiers and are checked by an automated de-identification gate before they leave the device and again on the server. No health data is stored on our servers.

6. Your rights

Regardless of where you live, you can:

  • Access and export all your data in the app: Profile › My data › Export (JSON on your device).
  • Delete all your data in the app: Profile › My data › Delete all my data. This removes the data from the device and from your iCloud backup. Because our server holds no copy and the AI provider receives only de-identified requests, there is nothing further for us to delete; on request we will confirm this in writing.
  • Withdraw consent for health-screening data or for the AI service at any time under Profile › My consents.
  • Ask us to confirm whether we hold consumer health data about you, to obtain a list of third parties with whom we have shared it (see section 4), or to exercise any right by e-mail: ekdigitaltech@gmail.com. We respond within 45 days (Washington/Connecticut) and, where a shorter period applies (30 days under KVKK, one month under GDPR), within that period.
  • Appeal a refusal by replying to our answer; we will re-examine it within 45 days. Washington residents may also contact the Washington State Attorney General; Connecticut residents the Connecticut Attorney General; Nevada residents the Nevada Attorney General.

We will never discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

7. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the app's handling of health data changes and note the version and effective date at the top. Material changes are announced in the app before they take effect.
