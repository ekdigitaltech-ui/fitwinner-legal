Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Version: 1.5 · Effective: 13 November 2026 · Last updated: 13 November 2026 · Regulated entity: EK Digital Technologies OÜ, Sepapaja tn 6, 15551 Tallinn, Estonia (Äriregister 17280400)

This policy is published for residents of Washington (My Health My Data Act, RCW 19.373), Nevada (Senate Bill 370, NRS 603A) and Connecticut (Connecticut Data Privacy Act, consumer health data provisions), and for any other person who wants to know exactly how the FitWinner app handles consumer health data. It supplements our Privacy Policy and Privacy Notice. For consumer health data, this policy prevails over any inconsistent statement in those documents.

"Consumer health data" means personal information that is linked or reasonably linkable to you and that identifies your past, present or future physical or mental health status, including the categories listed in section 1.

1. Categories of consumer health data we collect, and why

FitWinner is a device-first app. Every category below is collected only with your separate, affirmative, opt-in consent given in the app (the health-screening consent and, separately, the body measurements, wellbeing and Apple Health consent; or, for information you type, by your own choice to enter it), and is stored on your device only. There is no iCloud or other cloud sync; a copy exists elsewhere only if you save a backup file yourself, in a place you choose.

We do not collect precise geolocation, contacts, photos, biometric identifiers, genetic data, advertising identifiers or government identifiers. We do not use geofencing. We do not knowingly collect consumer health data from anyone under 18; the app is rated 18+ and refuses registration below that age.

2. Categories of sources

  • You: what you type or answer in the app.
  • Apple Health on your device: only the data types you authorise in iOS Settings, read-only.

We do not purchase, license or otherwise obtain consumer health data from third parties, affiliates or data brokers. No third party collects consumer health data about you over time or across different apps, websites or online services through FitWinner.

3. Categories of consumer health data we share, and with whom

We do not sell consumer health data and have never sold it. We do not share it with advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers or affiliates (we have none).

Sharing happens only if, and only while, you give the separate "AI service abroad" consent in the app. That consent is distinct from your consent to collect the data. When it is active, a request is sent, at the moment you ask for an interpretation, through our cloud provider to our AI provider. Outside Türkiye we treat this request as consumer health data that we disclose to our contracted service providers (processors), Supabase and Anthropic, which act only on our instructions; it is not anonymous:

Anthropic, PBC (USA) AI model provider, processor the request, to produce the interpretation; may retain inputs for a limited period for abuse monitoring under its own policy

Categories of consumer health data shared in that request:

  • your daily form-score series and its components — deviations from your own 28-day baseline, including, if you connected Apple Health, how your resting heart rate and heart-rate variability deviate from that baseline (the measurements themselves are not sent) — and your training-load ratio;
  • summaries of your last 28 days of training (date, type, duration, effort — for Apple Watch workouts effort may be estimated from heart rate), your planned sessions and calendar events (date, type; the title for the plan rationale);
  • your goal, sport, experience level and language;
  • your optional check-in note (at most 280 characters; only if you wrote one and also gave the health-screening consent);
  • if your health screening contains at least one flag, only a single generic "at least one health restriction" indicator — which condition it is (pregnancy, illness, medication, eating behaviour) is not sent;
  • for food recognition, the food name and portion you typed plus your last 20 food names.

Before the request leaves your phone, your name, email, user identifier and any persistent device identifier are removed (the app does not collect your name or email in the first place). In every country, names of people and places in the note and event titles, and contact details in any text, are replaced with placeholders on your device and never leave it (the masking is rule-based and may not catch every spelling). The request carries no name, email, user identifier, persistent device identifier, age, sex, height, weight, heart-rate readings or calorie target. Outside Türkiye, the app also obtains a one-time Apple DeviceCheck token from Apple to prove that the request comes from the genuine FitWinner app; our server only has Apple verify it and does not store it, and the token carries no health data, content or identity. For users in Türkiye, the note and event titles are not sent at all, dates in the training-form read and the plan rationale are shifted, the form-score series is shortened and rounded, capitalised words (names of people and brands) in the food text are replaced with placeholders, recent food names are not sent and no DeviceCheck token is used; their request first passes, in transit only, through our relay server in Istanbul (hosted by İHS Kurumsal Teknoloji Hizmetleri Anonim Şirketi, Türkiye), which drops the IP address and records neither the content nor the IP address. Our server keeps no copy of the request. We do not attempt to re-identify the request, and Supabase and Anthropic are bound by their data processing terms with us to process it only on our instructions and only to provide their service to us.

If you write to us by email, your message (your name, email address and the content of the correspondence, which may include health information you choose to mention) is stored in our mailbox at info@ekdigital.tech, hosted by Hostinger International Ltd. (Cyprus, EU) as our processor, and kept for 3 years after your request or question is closed. The app itself never sends us email.

We do not transmit consumer health data to Apple; the DeviceCheck token described above carries none. The app does not use iCloud. If you use your iPhone's own device backup, Apple provides that backup to you under your Apple Account.

We do not use your personal data, and do not allow it to be used, to train large language models. We do not process personal data for targeted advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use profiling to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you; there is therefore nothing to opt out of. If this ever changes, we will offer an opt-out before it starts.

4. Retention

On your device: until you delete it or delete the app. Backup files you save under Profile › My data: wherever you put them, until you delete them; we hold no copy. Our servers: the AI request itself is not stored. When an AI output is corrected or blocked by our safety rules, the safety audit trail keeps the name of the rule and, if the text was rewritten, the corrected AI text (which may contain health-related wording, but no name, user identifier, IP address or device data); it is deleted after 90 days. The abuse counter, which holds a daily salted hash of the IP address rather than the address itself, is deleted after at most 2 days. Supabase keeps its own service logs (including IP address and request time) for its short retention period. Email you send us: 3 years after your request or question is closed.

5. How we protect consumer health data

Data stays on your device under iOS app sandboxing; backup files you choose to save are password-protected by default (AES-256-GCM, key derived from your password), and we cannot read them or recover the password. Requests to our server carry no name, email, user identifier or persistent device identifier and pass an automated gate on the device and again on the server that rejects any request containing such fields.

6. Your rights and how to exercise them

You can, at any time and free of charge:

  • Confirm and access: see everything the app holds about you and export it as a file — Profile › My data › Export.
  • Delete: delete all your data — Profile › My data › Delete all my data and start over. This removes it from the device; backup files you saved elsewhere are yours to delete. Our servers hold no copy of your requests, and neither the stored audit trail nor our processors' records carry an identifier that would let us find your data in them, so there is nothing further we can locate and delete; Anthropic's short abuse-monitoring retention and Supabase's service logs expire on their own. On request we confirm this in writing within the periods below.
  • Correct: correct inaccurate information yourself at any time where you entered it in the app (for example under Profile or in the relevant log); if something cannot be edited there, email us and we will correct it.
  • Withdraw consent: for the health screening, for body measurements, wellbeing and Apple Health, or for the AI service — Profile › Your consents. Withdrawal takes effect immediately and deletes AI-generated text stored on your device. Withdrawing the health-screening consent deletes your health-screening answers. Withdrawing the body measurements, wellbeing and Apple Health consent deletes all your wellness check-in answers (including the note), all your weight entries, height, body-fat percentage, target weight and the data that came from Apple Health (heart-rate readings, effort estimated from heart rate, steps, active energy, sleep from Apple Health, weight from Apple Health) and stops the app reading Apple Health; your workout records stay, and your calorie target becomes a target you set yourself, starting from the last calculated value.
  • Obtain the list of third parties with whom your consumer health data has been shared: see section 3; we will also provide it on request.
  • Ask questions or exercise any right by email to info@ekdigital.tech (subject line "Health data request"). We answer within 45 days of receipt; if we need more time we tell you before the 45 days end and may take up to 45 additional days. Where a shorter period applies to you (30 days under KVKK, one month under GDPR) we respect it.
  • Appeal a decision by replying to our answer within 45 days; we re-examine it and respond in writing within 45 days, including how to contact your state attorney general: Washington State Office of the Attorney General (atg.wa.gov/file-complaint), Nevada Office of the Attorney General (ag.nv.gov), Connecticut Office of the Attorney General (portal.ct.gov/ag).

We will not deny goods or services, charge different prices or provide a different level of service because you exercised a right.

7. Changes to this policy

We change this policy when the app's handling of consumer health data changes and update the version, effective date and "last updated" line at the top. Material changes are announced in the app before they take effect and, where required, we ask for your consent again.
